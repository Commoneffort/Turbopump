1 · Who is who

Two places, five kinds of players

Our users trade on Turbopump. The vault, which holds depositors' money, takes the other side of every trade. The only thing we would ever trade on Hyperliquid is the hedge account. Leaders are people on Hyperliquid we only watch.

    6 · Income for depositors · added Oct 29

    New fees: what they add for depositors

    Market

    average of all runsworst run to best run$0

    Built and live on X1 testnet and devnet

    • Holding fee. When the vault's position on a market passes 10% of its limit, the crowded side pays a fee for every hour it holds, rising to 0.01% an hour at the limit (about 88% a year). Under 10%, nobody pays.
    • Crowded-side fee. 0.05% extra on any trade that makes the vault's position bigger. Closing your own position never pays it.
    • Liquidation fee. 0.5% of what a liquidation closes.
    • All of it goes to depositors. What a trader owes counts against their margin right away and is paid into the vault before they can withdraw.

    Not built, and why

    • Profit share (10% of copier profits, 40% of that to depositors): only about $100 per two weeks for depositors.
    • Part of the copy fee to depositors: the fee is in XNT and the vault holds USDC, so it needs a swap.
    • Staking part of the vault: the vault holds USDC, which can't be staked, and X1 unstaking waits about one epoch (~22 h).
